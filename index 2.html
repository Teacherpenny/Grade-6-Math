How to play

Teach slides have no points. Press Space to reveal one step at a time.

Question rounds:

  1. Read the problem. Everyone solves on a mini whiteboard.
  2. Say “3-2-1” and show boards. Press Space to reveal the setup, then the answer.
  3. Give points to racers who got it right (+ buttons or keys 1 to 4).
  4. The racer furthest along when time is up wins.

Points: Convert = 1. Divide: Level 1 = 1, Level 2 = 2, Level 3 = 3. Average speed: Level 1 = 2, Level 2 = 3. Click a chip at the top to jump to that part.
